# Commercial adoption paths

The open-source core remains local-first and deterministic. Commercial work can focus on
adoption rather than withholding correctness features:

- agent-release architecture reviews;
- behavioral-contract and risk-taxonomy design;
- migration from ad hoc eval suites;
- private MCP, trace, and policy adapters;
- CI rollout and evidence-retention design;
- enterprise support and compatibility commitments.

Potential hosted layers should be validated by external demand before implementation. A
dashboard, multi-tenant service, or policy marketplace is not part of the release candidate.
